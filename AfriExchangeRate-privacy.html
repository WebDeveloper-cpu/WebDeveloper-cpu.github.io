At AfriExchangeRate Calculator, accessible from our web application, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by AfriExchangeRate Calculator and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect and Process

AfriExchangeRate Calculator is built to respect user privacy. We do not require account creation, registration, or personal identification details (such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers) to use our application.

  • Client-Side Financial Data: All financial inputs, including currency conversion values, investment calculations, and loan/EMI estimates, are processed directly within your web browser. This data is never sent to our servers or stored in any database.
  • Device Preference Data (Local Storage): The application uses your browser's localStorage solely to remember user preferences, such as your preferred theme mode (Dark/Light Mode). This data remains locally on your device and is not transmitted anywhere.

2. Live Data Sources & Third-Party APIs

To provide live exchange rate conversions, our application connects to external public API services:

  • ExchangeRate-API (for fiat currency exchange rates)
  • CoinGecko API (for cryptocurrency market rates)

When your browser makes an API request to fetch updated exchange rates, your IP address and standard network request headers are transmitted to these third-party providers as part of standard HTTPS communication. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for details on how they handle network request logs.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Network Services

Our application includes third-party advertisements provided by services such as Monetag to support free access to our software.

  • Monetag & Ad Networks: Third-party ad networks or ad servers use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons in their respective advertisements and links that appear on AfriExchangeRate Calculator. These tools automatically receive your IP address when you interact with or view advertisements to measure campaign effectiveness or personalize advertising content.
  • External Links: Clicking on sponsored banner links or external ad links will direct you to external sites operated by third parties. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

4. Hosting Infrastructure

AfriExchangeRate Calculator is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit the website, GitHub may collect technical logs, including your web browser request, IP address, operating system, and timestamp for security, abuse prevention, and infrastructure debugging purposes. For more information, please review the GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Cookies and Web Storage

AfriExchangeRate Calculator does not set tracking or profiling cookies directly. However:

  • Third-party vendors (such as monetization/ad partners) may place cookies on your device to serve ads or collect anonymized web traffic statistics.
  • Local Storage is strictly used for storing local preferences (such as user interface themes).

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options or clear your web storage at any time via your browser settings.

6. Children's Information

AfriExchangeRate Calculator does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately, and we will do our best efforts to promptly remove such information.

7. Financial Disclaimer

AfriExchangeRate Calculator is an informational and educational tool designed to provide financial estimations, currency conversions, investment growth predictions, and loan calculations. Exchange rates and estimates are provided for general reference only and should not be construed as legal or financial advice. Always verify rates with official financial institutions before executing real transactions.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
