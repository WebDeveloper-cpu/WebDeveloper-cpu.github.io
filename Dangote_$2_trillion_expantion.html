Dangote’s Mega Refinement Plan: Workforce to Double as Capacity Eyes 1.4 Million Barrels Daily Target

Published: September 2026 Total Views: 0

LAGOS, NIGERIA — In a major development for Africa's energy sector, the Dangote Petroleum Refinery and Petrochemicals FZE has announced plans to double its current workforce as part of an aggressive expansion strategy aimed at boosting its crude refining capacity from 700,000 barrels per day (bpd) to 1.4 million bpd by 2029.

Devakumar Edwin, Group Vice President of Oil & Gas and Fertiliser at Dangote Industries Limited, disclosed the expansion targets during an inspection tour of the complex. He confirmed that basic engineering for the phase-two expansion has been completed, with detailed technical designs finalized and critical equipment already ordered from international manufacturers.

The ambitious expansion project coincides with the company's ongoing Initial Public Offering (IPO) on the Nigerian Exchange (NGX), which seeks to raise approximately ₦2.15 trillion to fund capital expenditures and scale petrochemical operations, including linear alkyl benzene and polypropylene units.

President of Dangote Group, Aliko Dangote, highlighted that the refinery complex is positioned to become the largest commercial entity on the African continent by the end of 2026. Operating with over 70% automation, the facility is configured to process diverse global crude grades and maximize high-value Euro 5 and Euro 6 standard fuels for both domestic consumption and international exports to Europe, the United States, and regional markets.
