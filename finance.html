Help & Guide

💱 Currency Exchange

Convert between African currencies, major world currencies, Bitcoin and USDT using live rates.

  • Enter the amount you want to convert
  • Select the From and To currencies
  • Tap Convert Live to get the latest rate
  • Use the Swap button to quickly reverse currencies

📈 Investment Calculator

Estimate how much your money can grow with compound interest.

  • Enter your starting principal
  • Enter the expected annual return rate (%)
  • Enter the number of years
  • Tap Calculate Future Value

🏦 Loan / EMI Calculator

Calculate your monthly loan repayment (EMI).

  • Enter the loan amount
  • Enter the annual interest rate (%)
  • Enter the loan duration in months
  • Tap Calculate Monthly Payment

📡 Offline Mode

If you lose internet connection, a banner will appear. Live currency rates require internet, but the Investment and Loan calculators continue to work offline.

🌙 Dark Mode

Tap the Dark / Light button in the top right to switch themes. Your preference is saved automatically.

About

Currency Exchange Hub

A simple, fast and free tool designed especially for African users to convert currencies, plan investments and calculate loan payments.

Features

  • Live currency conversion (fiat + crypto)
  • Support for major African currencies
  • Investment growth calculator
  • Loan / EMI calculator
  • Dark mode
  • Works offline (except live rates)
  • No account required

Data Sources

Live exchange rates are provided by:

  • ExchangeRate-API (fiat currencies)
  • CoinGecko (cryptocurrencies)

Disclaimer

This app is for informational and educational purposes only. Exchange rates and calculations should not be considered as financial advice. Always verify rates with official sources before making financial decisions.
