Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Universal Document, Image & PDF Converter mobile web application (the "App"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use our application.

1. Local Processing & File Privacy

Your privacy is our highest priority. All document, image, PDF, and text conversions occur strictly inside your device's web browser session using client-side JavaScript processing.

  • No files, documents, or images uploaded to the App are ever transmitted to, stored on, or processed by external web servers.
  • All processing happens locally in real-time. Once you close or refresh your app session, imported temporary files are cleared from browser memory.

2. External Services & Third-Party Content

While local file conversion operates completely offline, certain optional features or operational components interact with third-party services:

  • Google Docs Links: If you use the Google Doc URL feature, the App loads a preview iframe directly from docs.google.com. Your interactions with Google Docs are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Content Delivery Networks (CDNs): The App utilizes CDN libraries (such as Cloudflare CDN) solely to fetch static open-source conversion assets (e.g., PDF.js, Mammoth.js). CDNs may collect standard web logs (IP address, user agent) strictly for network performance and security auditing.
  • Sponsor & Promotional Links: The App contains clear, policy-compliant partner offer links. Clicking these links redirects you to external third-party partner websites, which operate under their own independent privacy policies.

3. Data Collection & Analytics

We do not track, store, or sell personal identifiers, user data, or conversion histories. We do not use third-party analytics SDKs to track individual user behavior inside the App.

4. Security

The App enforces a strict Content Security Policy (CSP) to restrict data flow solely to verified static library providers and official document previews. Because file conversions remain entirely local to your device, your private files are safeguarded against unauthorized interception during conversion.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. The utility is completely safe for general audiences.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to maintain compliance with app store guidelines or security standards. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our official repository support on GitHub or through our developer email listed in the app listing.
